Grounded Hill Country Living.

Finding or selling a home along the I-10 corridor is about far more than square footage—it is about aligning your lifestyle, your family's routine, and your long-term wealth. Serving Boerne, Fair Oaks Ranch, Cross Mountain, Leon Springs, and the Scenic Loop corridor, Stephanie Scott provides clear, high-touch advisory representation tailored to move-up buyers and empty nesters transitioning to their next chapter.

The Educator's Edge in Real Estate

Before guiding clients through multi-faceted real estate decisions, Stephanie spent years in the classroom and educational leadership as an elementary and middle school teacher. That experience translates into an unmatched advantage for her clients:

  • Micro-Market School Intelligence: School zoning changes the rhythm of your week. Stephanie understands the distinct cultures, zoning boundaries, and commute dynamics of Boerne ISD, Fair Oaks Ranch schools, and private academic options like the Geneva School of Boerne.
  • Commute-Tested Guidance: From test-driving neighborhood routes at peak traffic hours for out-of-town buyers to analyzing IH-10 access points along Toutant Beauregard and Boerne Stage Road, Stephanie removes the guesswork before you write an offer.
  • Calm, Strategic Advocacy: Contract negotiations, option periods, and appraisal contingencies require the same traits that defined her teaching career: clear communication, rapid response times, and patient, steadfast advocacy.

Strategic Wealth-Building for Move-Up Buyers & Downsizers
