Buying Acreage Homes & Custom Builds in the Hill Country.

Navigating the complexities of land, zoning, and rural luxury.

There is a unique draw to the Texas Hill Country—the sprawling live oaks, the dramatic topography, and the privacy of acreage living. However, buying a custom home on raw land, or purchasing an existing acreage estate along the Scenic Loop corridor, Cross Mountain, or Toutant Beauregard, requires specialized real estate expertise.

The Complexities of Acreage Real Estate

Purchasing land is vastly different from buying a home in a standard subdivision. When we evaluate acreage properties, my advisory services extend into heavily technical areas that protect your investment:

  • Well Water and Septic Systems: Understanding Edwards Aquifer regulations, well depths, flow rates, and the maintenance history of aerobic vs. conventional septic systems.
  • ETJ (Extraterritorial Jurisdiction): Many homes in the Hill Country sit outside formal city limits but within an ETJ, impacting taxes, city services, and future development rights.
  • Topography and Build-ability: If you are buying a lot for a future custom build, we must assess slope, flood plains, and the hidden costs of land clearing and foundation work on rocky Hill Country terrain.

Gated Acreage Communities vs. Unrestricted Land

Are you looking for the protection of an HOA with architectural controls, or do you want unrestricted land where you can build a workshop and keep livestock? Communities like Fair Oaks Ranch and Anaqua Springs offer highly curated luxury living on acreage, while outer Boerne Stage Road offers more flexible, unrestricted tracts. I help you define your lifestyle parameters to find the perfect fit.

Strategic Advisory for Sellers of Acreage Estates

If you are selling an acreage property, standard comparable sales models (comps) often fail. A 5-acre lot with a view and mature oaks values entirely differently than a 5-acre flat tract. I utilize disciplined market analysis, high-end drone photography, and targeted digital marketing to position your acreage estate to the right demographic of luxury buyers.
